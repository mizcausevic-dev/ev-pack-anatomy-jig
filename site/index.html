Anatomy of an EV battery pack under fast charge

A 34-second, 1,020-frame cutaway: sealed exterior, ballistic shield lift, serpentine cold-plate flow, a sliced jelly-roll cell with BMS sense traces, and an exploded isometric view of the high-voltage series path closing on the contactors. No keyframe files, no imported models. Every frame is computed from its frame number.

How it is built

One pure function owns the timeline. The 3D scene and the HTML overlay both read from it, and the callout leader lines project the same 3D points through the same camera math, so labels stay pinned to the geometry while the camera moves.

src/timeline.ts   shot table, spherical camera keys (log-lerped radius), sceneAt(frame)
src/geometry.ts   pack layout, serpentine curve, exploded transforms, HV series chain
src/Scene.tsx     instanced cells, cold plates, busbars, BMS, hero cell, junction box
src/Hud.tsx       titles, timeline, projected callouts, telemetry panel

On-screen telemetry (cell voltage, temperatures, state of charge, the 398.4 V pack total) is illustrative and time-compressed. It does not come from any real battery pack.
